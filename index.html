Underlying Chemistry

Reactions & Surfactant Kinetics

How surfactants, acids, and humectants behave on a molecular level during washing.

01

Critical Micelle Concentration (CMC) & Dirt Trapping

SLS Monomers (>CMC) + Sebum/Dirt → Spherical Micelle Complex

Above the CMC, SLS molecules self-assemble into spheres. Non-polar tails point inwards to encapsulate insoluble grease, while ionic heads interact with water to rinse it away.

02

Citric Acid Tri-Protoc Equilibrium & Buffering

C₆H₈O₇ + H₂O ⇌ C₆H₇O₇⁻ + H₃O⁺ (pKa₁ = 3.13)

Citric acid buffers against alkalinity. By donating hydronium ions, it counters the high pH of raw surfactants, preserving the skin's acidic lipid bilayer against opportunistic bacteria.

03

BHA Keratolytic Exfoliation Mechanism

Salicylic Acid (Lipophilic) + Keratin Bridges → Desmosome Cleavage

Because salicylic acid is oil-soluble, it dissolves past surface sebum and hydrolyzes intracellular adhesive desmosomes between stratum corneum squames.

04

Glycerine Hydrogen Bonding Matrix

Glycerol (3 -OH Groups) + 3 H₂O → Hydrated Polyol Complex

The three hydroxyl groups in glycerol form coordinate hydrogen bonds with water clusters, slowing trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL) post cleanse.

Phase 1: Weighing

Precision Weighing & Reagent Calibration

Distilled water (250 mL) was measured into a sterile 500 mL borosilicate beaker as the liquid foundation. Using medical-grade graduated syringes, precise quantities of ethylene glycol and glycerine were dispensed to ensure exact molar ratios.

Base Volume: 250 mL H₂O
Tolerance: ±0.05 mL
Glassware: 500 mL Beaker

Phase 2: Active Preparation

Salicylic Acid Solubilization & Filtration

Salicylic acid crystals are hydrophobic and insoluble in cold water alone. The acid was pre-dissolved in ethylene glycol at 40°C with continuous magnetic stirring. The resultant golden solution was filtered through Whatman filter paper to eliminate any undissolved particulates.

Temperature: 40°C Mild Heat
Solvent: Ethylene Glycol
Clarity: 100% Optical Translucency

Phase 3: Surfactant Matrix

Surfactant Addition (SLS Incorporation)

Sodium Lauryl Sulfate powder was introduced incrementally into the water phase under low-shear mixing (120 RPM). Rapid agitation was avoided to prevent excessive aeration and entrapment of air bubbles, yielding a stable uniform micellar dispersion.

Stirring Speed: 120 RPM (Low Shear)
Concentration: ~15% w/v
Foam Control: Laminar Flow

Phase 4: Homogenization

Sequential Emulsification & Aloe Vera Infusion

The pre-dissolved active phase (salicylic acid + glycol) was blended into the surfactant base, followed by glycerine and pure aloe vera gel. Mixing was sustained for 15 minutes until complete rheological stability and silky consistency were achieved.

Phase 5: Quality Control

pH Titration with Citric Acid & Final Formulation

Citric acid solution was titrated dropwise into the formulation while testing with calibrated pH strips. Upon achieving equilibrium at pH 5.0 (natural skin mantle), the bodywash was decanted for clarity, foam stability testing, and storage in pump containers.

Stage Details

1
Phase 1: Base Mixing

Distilled Water, Ethylene Glycol, Glycerin, and Citric Acid are combined into an initial aqueous solvent phase.

2
Phase 2: Raw Solution Formation

Primary surfactant (SLS) and lipid active (Salicylic Acid) feed directly into the base to form the intermediate Raw Solution.

3
Phase 3: Thermal Dissolution & Blending

The Raw Solution undergoes second-stage mixing and controlled heating (40°C) to completely dissolve salicylic acid crystals.

4
Phase 4: Botanical Infusion & Filtration

Pure Aloe Vera gel feeds into the filtration stage to preserve natural enzymes, discharging the finished crystal-clear Body Wash.

Real-World Assessment

Applications, Advantages & Limitations

A critical engineering evaluation of the formulation's clinical utility and industrial scaling viability.

🎯

Target Applications

  • Daily Body Cleansing: Balanced daily wash removing pollution, sweat, and sebum.
  • Acne-Prone Skin Therapy: 1.5% salicylic acid exfoliates follicular keratosis (bacne & chest acne).
  • Post-Workout Antiseptic: Washes away sweat-borne gym bacteria while glycerine maintains skin hydration.
  • Dermatological Scalp/Body Cleanser: Reduces dead skin flaking and clears sebum build-up.
  • Hospital Sanitizing Formulation Base: Non-drying antiseptic carrier for institutional personal care.
✅

Formula Advantages

  • Physiological pH Equilibrium (5.0): Prevents the alkaline lipid breakdown caused by standard soap bars (pH 9-10).
  • Botanical Soothing Action: Pure aloe vera gel counteracts surfactant drying and calms inflammation.
  • Dual Active System: Keratolytic BHA action combined with triple-hydroxyl humectant moisture trapping.
  • Readily Sourced Raw Materials: Economical benchtop formula scalable to pilot batch production.
  • High Biodegradability: Surfactants and organic acids break down safely in wastewater streams.
⚠️

Limitations & Mitigations

  • SLS Sensitivity in Atopic Skin: May trigger mild erythema in eczema-prone individuals; mitigated by soothing aloe vera.
  • Salicylic Acid Caution: High concentrations contraindicated during pregnancy; benchtop batch capped at mild cosmetic dosage.
  • Absence of Broad Preservative: Fresh botanical formulation requires refrigeration or paraben-free booster for 12+ month shelf life.
  • Precise pH Sensitivity: Viscosity and active solubility degrade if pH drifts beyond 4.5–5.5 range.
